# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CALATRAVA is used by Coastal Debt Resolve employees, managers, HR staff, administrators, recruiters, and operations leaders. Users work in a busy office environment and need to complete HR tasks quickly, understand who can see sensitive information, and trust that records, notifications, and external integrations reflect the correct person.

## Product Purpose

CALATRAVA brings recruiting, employee records, onboarding, offboarding, reviews, calendars, documents, communication, and HR reporting into one internal system. Success means routine HR work is completed in one place with clear ownership, accurate permissions, reliable notifications, and minimal manual coordination.

## Brand Personality

Clear, trustworthy, and efficient. The voice is direct and helpful. It uses familiar HR language, explains consequences before an action, and avoids unnecessary technical terms.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product feel like a promotional website, playful consumer calendar, or experimental design demo. Avoid decorative effects, surprising navigation, dense walls of controls, vague labels, and interactions that hide permissions or consequences.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the task visible. Primary actions and current state should be immediately understandable.
2. Make ownership explicit. Organizers, assignees, audiences, and permission boundaries should never be ambiguous.
3. Use progressive disclosure. Show the common path first and reveal advanced scheduling or audience controls when needed.
4. Confirm external effects. Clearly report when emails, invitations, calendar events, or account changes are created.
5. Preserve familiar patterns. Extend the existing CALATRAVA component language instead of introducing a separate visual system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, understandable error messages, and status cues that do not rely on color alone.
